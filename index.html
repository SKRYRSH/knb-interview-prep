Interview Tips — UM Specific

🕐
Log in 15 minutes early. They explicitly said this. Be in the Zoom waiting room by 11:15 AM GMT+7 (9:45 AM India time). A late entry is instant disqualification.
👔
Wear formal attire — full upper body. Button-up shirt minimum. No casual T-shirts. The camera sees your shoulders up, but dress fully formal to feel confident.
🌐
Test your internet the night before. Use speedtest.net. Run a Zoom test call. If your connection is unstable, use a hotspot as backup — have it ready.
📷
Camera at eye level. Don't look down at a laptop on a table. Stack books under it. Eye contact = confidence.
🔇
Silent room, no interruptions. Tell everyone in your house. Put your phone on silent. Close unnecessary browser tabs. Use headphones with a mic for clear audio.
🇮🇩
Say a word or two in Bahasa Indonesia. Even "Selamat pagi, terima kasih banyak" at the start or end. It shows cultural effort and they love it.
⏱
Keep answers to 90-120 seconds max. Interviewers ask 3 panelists' worth of questions in limited time. Be concise, clear, confident. Don't ramble.
📝
Have notes — but don't read from them. Keep key bullet points visible off-screen. Glance only if you freeze. Maintain eye contact with the camera, not your notes.
🧘
Pause before answering. A 2-3 second pause after a question looks like you're thinking — not panicking. Never rush to fill silence.
💬
English is preferred, Bahasa Indonesia is fine. Answer in the language you're most comfortable and articulate in. Don't try Bahasa if you'll stumble — fluent English beats broken Bahasa.
❓
Prepare 1-2 questions to ask them. At the end they'll say "any questions?" Have something ready: "What does a typical academic day look like for international students at UM?" This shows genuine interest.
🌟
They already liked you enough to interview you. You passed the initial screening. Walk in with that confidence. They're not trying to trick you — they want to see if you're the right fit.
